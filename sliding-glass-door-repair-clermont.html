Sliding glass door repair in Clermont

Clermont has grown fast, especially south toward US-27, so a lot of the doors we see are in newer homes with impact glass and big multi-panel sliders. Hilly lots and soil movement can throw a door out of alignment, and the afternoon sun is hard on double-pane seals. We cover Clermont as part of our regular route.

What we see in Clermont

  • Doors knocked out of alignment that rub the frame or won't lock
  • Foggy double-pane glass from seals worn out by the sun
  • Large impact sliders that need rollers rated for their weight

What we fix in Clermont

  • Sliding door repair — Doors that stick, drag, jump the track or are hard to push.
  • Roller replacement — Worn rollers swapped so the door glides again.
  • Track repair — Dented, worn or corroded tracks resurfaced or capped.
  • Locks & handles — Latches that won't catch, broken handles, extra security locks.
  • Broken glass replacement — Cracked or shattered panels replaced with safety glass cut to size.
  • Foggy glass replacement — Double-pane glass fogged between the panes, replaced.
  • Sliding door panel & glass replacement — Damaged panels or glass replaced in your existing frame.
  • Patio door glass repair — Loose glass, worn glazing and seals on patio doors.
  • Commercial glass — Glass for offices and businesses, replaced in existing frames.
  • Office glass partitions — Tempered panels for office partitions and interior glass walls.
  • Tempered glass — Tempered safety glass made to size for doors and partitions.
  • Laminated glass — Laminated glass for impact doors, security and less noise.

How a repair goes

  1. Send a photo or call

    WhatsApp a photo or video of the door, or call. We'll ask a few questions and give you an idea of what's going on.

  2. Free estimate at your home

    The technician measures, checks the rollers, track and glass, and gives you the exact price before touching anything.

  3. Repair, usually the same visit

    Our van carries common rollers, locks and hardware, so most door repairs are finished on the first trip. Glass is ordered to size.

Questions we get a lot

Do you charge extra to come to Clermont?

No. Clermont is in our regular service area, and the on-site estimate is free.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. The technician comes out, checks the door and gives you the exact price. If you decide not to go ahead, you don't pay anything.

Can you quote glass over the phone?

No — glass depends on the exact size and whether it's single or double pane, so we measure first. Rollers run $65–$89 each depending on the model.
