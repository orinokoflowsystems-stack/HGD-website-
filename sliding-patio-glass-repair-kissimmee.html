Sliding patio door glass repair in Kissimmee

Patio door glass in Kissimmee deals with sun, storms, kids and pets. We figure out whether the glass needs replacing or just needs to be re-secured and resealed — loose panes, worn glazing and failed weatherstripping can often be fixed without new glass.

Signs you need this

  • Glass that rattles or shifts in the panel
  • Gaps, drafts or water getting in around the glass
  • Glazing strips or seals that are cracked or missing
  • Cracks, chips or fog in the glass itself

What we do on the visit

  • Check the glass, glazing and seals on each panel
  • Re-secure loose glass and replace worn glazing
  • Replace weatherstripping where air or water gets in
  • Replace cracked or fogged glass when it can't be repaired

Pricing

Glazing and seal repairs are quoted at the door; new glass is priced after measuring. The estimate is free.

Questions we get a lot

Do you do patio door glass repair in Kissimmee?

Yes. Kissimmee (Osceola County) is in our regular service area, there's no extra trip charge and the on-site estimate is free.

Can loose glass be fixed without replacing it?

Usually yes. If the glass itself is intact, new glazing and seals hold it firmly again.

Water comes in at the bottom of my door — is that the glass?

Sometimes it's the seals around the glass, sometimes the track drainage. We check both.
