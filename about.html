About Handy Glass & Door

Handy Glass & Door is a local company that does one thing: we repair sliding glass doors and replace the glass in them. Rollers, tracks, locks, handles, broken glass and foggy double-pane units — that's the whole list.

We started because most handymen don't want to touch a heavy glass panel, and most glass companies want to sell you a new door. We think most doors are worth fixing, and we'd rather tell you that honestly than sell you something you don't need.

We work across Central Florida, from Orlando and Kissimmee to Windermere, Lake Nona and Polk County. We speak English and Spanish, and we'll talk to you in whichever you prefer.

What you can count on

  • A free estimate at your home, with the price before the work
  • No hidden fees — the price we give you is the price you pay
  • Warranty on the glass, rollers and hardware we install
  • A technician who cleans up and tests the door with you before leaving
