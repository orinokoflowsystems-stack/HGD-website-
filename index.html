What we fix

Six things, done well. Everything here is about sliding glass doors and the glass in them.

  • Sliding door repair

    Doors that stick, drag, jump the track or are hard to push.

  • Roller replacement

    Worn rollers swapped so the door glides again.$65–$89

  • Track repair

    Dented, worn or corroded tracks resurfaced or replaced.

  • Locks & handles

    Latches that won't catch, broken handles, extra security locks.

  • Broken glass replacement

    Cracked or shattered panels replaced with safety glass cut to size.

  • Foggy & insulated glass

    Double-pane glass fogged between the panes, replaced.

How a repair goes

  1. Send a photo or call

    WhatsApp a photo or video of the door, or call. We'll ask a few questions and give you an idea of what's going on.

  2. Free estimate at your home

    The technician measures, checks the rollers, track and glass, and gives you the exact price before touching anything.

  3. Repair, usually the same visit

    Our van carries common rollers, locks and hardware, so most door repairs are finished on the first trip. Glass is ordered to size.
