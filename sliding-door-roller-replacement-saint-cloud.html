Sliding door roller replacement in Saint Cloud

When a sliding door in Saint Cloud gets heavy, grinds or scrapes the track, the rollers are usually done. We swap them for steel or stainless rollers matched to your door, adjust the height and clean the track — normally in one visit.

Signs you need this

  • The door got heavier to push over time
  • Grinding or bumps as it slides
  • The bottom of the panel scrapes the track
  • Rust or broken plastic wheels under the door

What we do on the visit

  • Remove the panel and pull the old rollers
  • Match replacements to your door's brand and size
  • Install steel or stainless rollers and set the height
  • Clean the track so the new rollers last

Pricing

Rollers are $65–$89 each depending on the model, plus $175–$200 labor per visit no matter how many panels. We confirm the exact total at your door; the estimate is free.

Questions we get a lot

Do you do roller replacement in Saint Cloud?

Yes. Saint Cloud (Osceola County) is in our regular service area, there's no extra trip charge and the on-site estimate is free.

How long do new rollers last?

With a clean track, years. Stainless rollers hold up best around pools and lanais.

Can I just spray lubricant on the track?

Don't use oil or WD-40 — it collects sand. A dry silicone spray on a clean track is fine.
