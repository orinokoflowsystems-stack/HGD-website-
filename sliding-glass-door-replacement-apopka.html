Sliding glass door replacement — panels & glass in Apopka

Thinking of replacing a sliding glass door in Apopka? If the frame is still solid, we replace just the damaged panel or its glass and keep the rest — usually much less cost and mess than a whole new door. We'll check the frame first and tell you honestly if it's beyond saving.

  • A panel that's bent, cracked or badly damaged

  • Inspect the frame, track and both panels

Questions we get a lot

Do you do sliding door panel & glass replacement in Apopka?

Yes. Apopka (Orange County) is in our regular service area, there's no extra trip charge and the on-site estimate is free.

Do you replace the whole door frame?

No. We replace panels and glass in your existing frame. If the frame itself is failing, we'll tell you so you can plan a full replacement.

Is replacing a panel cheaper than a new door?

In most cases yes, because the frame, trim and wall around it stay untouched.

Can I call to book at night or on weekends?

Yes. Our phone lines take bookings 24/7 in English and Spanish at (407) 561-9844 and (407) 379-5085. Appointments are Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM.
